# Spokewise ART BRIEF

Dark graphite workshop with cold anodised teal accents, pinned Dark. Background `#0E1418`, text `#DCE6EA`, drive-side teal `#4FD1C5`, non-drive `#2E7A74`, rim-deviation polygon `#F2C14E`, hairlines `#22323A` and `#1B2A31`, hub ring `#2E4650`.

The hero is a 32-spoke polar map: rim circle, dashed reference circle, hub ring, one line per spoke coloured by flange, and the closed yellow rim-deviation polygon over the top. Lens chips are fully rounded; readouts are monospaced teal. Secondary destinations sit in a quiet two-tier monospaced chrome with middots — not equal teal capsules. Avoid warm wax tones (reserved for Combwright) and purple gradients.
