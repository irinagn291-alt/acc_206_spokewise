## Description

Spokewise is an offline workshop for wheel builders. A 32-spoke polar map is the hero — switch Tension, Dish and True lenses over one artefact. Enter deflections around the wheel, compute spoke lengths to tenths of a millimetre from ERD and flange geometry, keep calibration tables, truing passes and build history.

Statistics show radial deviation and tension histograms. Compare two wheels, browse eighteen lacing patterns, and export a CSV tension sheet. No account, no network.
