# Spokewise — App Review Notes

Fully offline Sports utility. No accounts, IAP, ads or network.

1. Launch lands on the polar tension map with three lenses.
2. Pick a seeded wheel; use Rapid spoke entry, Spoke length, Components, Calibration, Truing, Builds, Statistics, Compare, Reference, Maintenance and Settings.
3. Settings exports a CSV tension sheet via ShareLink.

Demo data seeds four wheels with tension readings; one spoke is intentionally out of tolerance.
